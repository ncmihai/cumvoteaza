# Public UI audit — 13 September 2026

## Scope and evidence

Fresh production review of `https://cumvoteaza.vercel.app` in the Codex in-app browser at a 854 × 808 viewport. The pass covered Romanian home, vote directory, vote detail, bill directory, bill detail, member directory, member profile, party profile, current composition, composition history and data health, plus English homepage parity. Screenshots and accessibility-tree snapshots were captured during this run; filter dismissal and the homepage hot-vote carousel were exercised interactively.

This is a product and accessibility triage, not a claim of WCAG compliance. Browser console, network waterfalls, screen-reader output, touch devices and additional physical viewport sizes were not available in this pass.

## Phase 1 reconciliation update

The first two P0 consistency findings below have since been repaired. Member activity now uses one legislature-and-chamber-scoped read model, with important, standard and procedural vote counts kept separately. Current party seats now come from the same authoritative composition snapshot used by the seat map. A verified 13 September roster refresh produces exactly 330 deputy mandates and 134 senator mandates, with no duplicate current group memberships; PNL consequently resolves to 54 deputies and 26 senators on both surfaces. Historical overlapping memberships remain available for career timelines.

The current-government alignment finding remains open. Support/opposition totals must stay suppressed or explicitly unknown until the government interval and coalition mappings have verified source evidence.

## Prioritized findings

### P0 — trust and factual consistency

1. **The same member has conflicting vote totals.** The current member directory reports Adrian-Felician Cozma with 538 votes, while his profile reports 427 expressed votes. A civic-information product cannot make users infer which figure is authoritative.
2. **Party seat totals conflict across public pages.** Current composition reports PNL at 49 deputies and 22 senators, while the PNL profile reports 54 deputies and 23 senators for the same current legislature/date context.
3. **Current government context is internally implausible/incomplete.** The composition and bill/vote context call the current government “Bolojan interimar,” show no verified coalition mapping, classify inspected seats as “Necunoscut,” and calculate both governmental support and opposition as zero. Suppress those derived totals until classification is present, or repair the current-government interval and affiliation mapping.

### P1 — broken or misleading journey behavior

4. **Long detail-page headings overflow around tablet width.** At 854px, the member name, vote title and party name extend beyond the right edge. The affected templates use desktop-scale typography before enough horizontal room exists. Reduce the `md` heading size or move the larger type breakpoint to `lg`, and add a regression check at 768–1024px.
5. **Vote detail repeats a very large nominal dataset.** The page exposes a six-person preview, a 330-seat interactive map, a 291-row nominal table and repeated group summaries in one document. This creates thousands of accessibility nodes, excessive page length and unclear hierarchy. Keep one primary visualization, lazy-mount the full table, and expose the alternate view on demand.
6. **The party “Toate voturile” link is not actually all party votes.** On the PNL profile it targets `group-senate-pnl`, even though the profile summarizes both chambers. Either query all mapped PNL groups or label the link as Senate-only.
7. **Bill detail leaks raw data-model values and importer formatting.** Public procedure rows display `unknown` and `deputies`; the initiator summary is concatenated (`deputati+senatori...`) and visually truncated. Localize enum values, format initiators as structured groups, and use a readable fallback for unknown provenance.

### P2 — accessibility, clarity and polish

8. **Vote-directory filters are unnamed to assistive technology.** The expanded panel exposes six unlabeled popup controls plus unlabeled reset/apply controls in the accessibility tree. Associate visible labels with each field and give both actions explicit accessible names.
9. **English navigation retains a Romanian accessible label.** The mobile menu button is announced as “Deschide meniul” on `/en`. Localize the control name and verify expanded/collapsed state text.
10. **Official Romanian titles are not framed in the English experience.** The surrounding UI is English while bill/vote names remain Romanian. Keeping source text is valid, but add an “official title in Romanian” label or an editorial English summary so the language switch has a clear contract.
11. **Public data health eagerly renders the whole review queue.** The accessibility tree contains thousands of nodes and internal repair commands. Paginate or virtualize by category; keep review controls collapsed and mount them only after an authorized review session begins.
12. **Canonical and alternate bill identifiers are easy to confuse.** A result routed as `/pl-x-404-2026` is headed `B750/2025` in the directory. Show both identifiers with chamber labels when the public route uses a different canonical identifier.
13. **Raw institutional offices still leak into some names outside profiles.** Vote rows and seat-map accessible names include strings such as “Vicepreşedinte Al Camerei Deputaţilor,” while the member profile correctly separates name and office. Reuse the normalized identity presentation across every surface.

## Strengths to preserve

- The homepage now has a working five-item hot-vote carousel, clear progress controls and a useful group-vote preview.
- Homepage and directory filter disclosures close on outside click, resolving the earlier sticky-overlay defect.
- Directory cards have much stronger hierarchy, consistent actions and honest official-source language.
- Historical composition is substantially cleaner than the earlier clustered version and keeps the full timeline optional.
- Member profiles separate sourced identity, career, activity and empty states without fabricating missing biographies or attendance percentages.
- The public data-health page makes known limitations visible instead of presenting incomplete records as certain.

## Recommended repair order

1. Reconcile member and party aggregate queries against a single legislature/as-of contract; repair or suppress current government alignment until verified.
2. Fix tablet-width overflow across member, party, vote and bill detail templates.
3. Simplify and lazy-load vote detail and data-health heavy sections.
4. Fix party vote routing, bill-detail presentation leaks and cross-surface identity normalization.
5. Complete accessible names and English localization, then run keyboard and screen-reader verification at mobile, tablet and desktop breakpoints.
